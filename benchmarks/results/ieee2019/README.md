# Compiled and kernel time of the operations of IEEE 754-2019

Every operation of IEEE 754-2019 clauses 5 and 9 that FloatLib provides, under its name in the standard, at the binary widths 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 1,024 and 4,096 bits and at the three decimal formats. Written by the timing mode of `benchmarks/plots/figures.py` on one shared machine (Intel Xeon Platinum 8275CL @ 3.00GHz, 96 threads, shared with other jobs); `../figures/measured.json` has the exit code and the time of every section. `n/a` is a cell without a result, and `< 1` a kernel cell under half a microsecond: the difference of two folds, which for the cheapest operations can come out at zero or below on a busy machine (the CSV keeps the number).

* Compiled time (`compiled.csv`), measured at `e58d3251` on 2026-11-18 (Lean v4.36.0): `execFloatIEEE2019` (`benchmarks/lean/FloatLibBenchmarks/Public/IEEE2019.lean`), nanoseconds for one call on the descriptor model `Model fmt` or on a decimal `Datum`, the fastest of five trials of at least 20 ms over 64 operand triples. The reductions take eight elements or pairs.
* The rows `exp2` to `rootn` of the binary compiled table, measured at `e58d3251` on 2026-11-18 (Lean v4.36.0): the recommended functions of clause 9.2 (`execFloatIEEE2019 functions FORMAT`), one pass over eight operands up to 128 bits, two operands at 256 bits and one at 1,024 and at 4,096 bits, where each function runs alone and has no result after 120 seconds.
* Kernel time (`kernel.csv`), measured at `e58d3251` on 2026-11-18 (Lean v4.36.0), the formats of 1,024 and 4,096 bits at `e58d3251` on 2026-11-18 (Lean v4.36.0): `benchmarks/kernel/ieee2019.sh 4 3`, microseconds for one evaluation by Lean's kernel on the configured type `ExecFloat.Binary` or on a decimal `Datum`: as written, and after the rewrite of `float_decide` with the rules tagged `@[kernel_optimize]`. A cell is the time of a fold over four operand triples minus the time of a fold that only reads and stores an operand, divided by four, the fastest of three. The reductions take three elements or two pairs. The decimal operands have full coefficients, so sums and products drop digits. At 1,024 and 4,096 bits every row runs in its own Lean process with a limit of 60 seconds, and a row that needs longer has none. The payload rows subtract their preparation.

The 8-bit format `e4f3` is the IEEE layout with four exponent bits and three fraction bits; `binary1024` and `binary4096` are the IEEE layouts with 27 and 35 exponent bits. The figure `kernel-ieee2019` of [`../FIGURES.md`](../FIGURES.md) draws the kernel tables by family, and `correctly-rounded` the recommended functions.
